Artist's Story

Chris is an artist specializing in traditional media, including gouache and oil painting, while also exploring digital illustration. Currently, they have been pursuing a career as an artist through commissioned work. Chris is eager to further enhance their skills in both traditional and digital mediums. Their lifelong pursuit of art stems not only from a passion for creation but also from a profound fascination with visual communication, enabling them to convey emotions and narratives that words often fail to express. Through their work, Chris focuses on capturing brief daily moments, using art as a means to share their perspective on the world and connect with others.
